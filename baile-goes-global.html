WHAT IS BAILE GOES GLOBAL?

Brazil is the
starting point.

Baile Goes Global is a Brazilian-rooted music platform and club night created by Moniki in New York City.

Built around the energy of baile funk, BGG connects Brazilian sounds with the music moving dance floors around the world, from Afro and Latin rhythms to house, bass, edits and global club music.

Each edition brings together resident and guest DJs with different backgrounds, influences and perspectives, creating a room where sounds and cultures meet without losing the Brazilian pulse at the center.

Born in Brooklyn, Baile Goes Global is growing beyond a party into a platform for music discovery, DJ culture, collaboration and exchange.
